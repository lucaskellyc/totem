# Totem v0.3.2-alpha

> ⚠️ **Alpha pre-release.** APIs may change without notice.

An experimental block-based 3D scene builder for [three.js](https://threejs.org/). This release removes the shader-compile stutter right after the loading screen, makes collapsed-mode paging easier to trigger, and adds two new extras: a gem material and a wireframe materialize effect.

**[Live demo](https://lucaskellyc.github.io/totem/)**

## Highlights

- 💎 **Gem material** — `type: "gem"` gives any mesh a faceted, hue-rotating iridescent look with studio reflections and twinkling facets, no environment map needed. The demo's stylish block now has a spinning diamond.
- 🕸️ **Materialize effect** — `materialize: { ... }` builds a mesh out of a glowing wireframe, holds, dissolves and repeats. It ships in `lib/extras/` but isn't enabled on any demo block yet.
- ⚡ **No more startup stutter** — shaders are warmed once every block is placed, the shadow-caster count no longer drifts, textures upload up front, and hidden columns are warmed behind the loading screen (55 → 3 programs linked after reveal on an M-series Mac).
- 👆 **Easier paging** — collapsed-mode swipes lock at up to ~55° off horizontal and page on shorter, slower flicks.

## Added

- **`lib/extras/gem.js`** — `GemEffect` class. Opt in via `type: "gem"` (with `color`, `hueSpeed`, `dispersion`, `shine`, `twinkle`) on any block or component spec. Dims off-focus with the other emissive glows.
- **`lib/extras/materialize.js`** — `MaterializeEffect` class. Opt in via `materialize: { ... }`; sweep angle, timing, loop, lead, ghost, quad recovery and noise `turbulence` are configurable. Apply it after the other effects.
- **`lib/extras/video.js`** — `VideoEffect.prime(timeout)` buffers and decodes the first frame of every in-focus video, then pauses.
- **`lib/totem/totem.js`** — `totem.ready` resolves once every block is placed; `totem.shadowBudget` (default 3) sets how many lights may cast shadows at once.
- Demo: a spinning gem diamond in the stylish block.

## Changed

- **`lib/totem/totem.js`** — the shadow-caster count is pinned to `shadowBudget` instead of following brightness, and point-light shadow cubes are drawn once and frozen, redrawn only when a caster joins the budget or a block wraps. **Shadow-casting meshes must not animate within their block.**
- **`lib/totem/totem.js`** — gem materials and LineSegments wireframes are tracked for the scroll/brightness fade.
- **`lib/totem/loaders.js`** — desktop maps are capped at 2048 px.
- **`lib/totem/gestures.js`** — `swipeBias` lets drags up to ~55° off horizontal lock as swipes.
- Demo: page at 15% of width or 0.2 px/ms (was 25% / 0.35), with a 450 ms post-page cooldown (was 700 ms); drop canvas MSAA; `BasicShadowMap` on mobile; page-dot and info-logo glass styling tweaks.

## Fixed

- Demo: heavy shader-compile stutter for the first seconds after the loading screen lifts.
- Demo: hitching the first time a hidden column pages into view.
